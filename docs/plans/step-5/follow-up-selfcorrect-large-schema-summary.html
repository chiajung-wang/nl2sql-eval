3 Why +0.050 is signal, not the noise floor

This is the distinction the twin metric exists to make:

  • The strong-model 0.725 vs 0.675 difference is between two independent runs → pure resampling jitter (~0.05 floor, temperature>0).
  • The weak-model +0.050 is within one run: pass@1 is derived from the same pass@3 run's first attempts, so the 2 recovered questions specifically errored on attempt 1 and the loop flipped them correct — recovery_rate + the +7 added attempts are the causal proof, not a second sample.

Honest limit (disclosed): the weak arm is n=1 at one k — a clean demonstration that the loop fires and recovers, not a precise magnitude; +0.050 is illustrative. Added dollar cost is unpriced (kimi routes via OpenRouter, which the twin's dated list table doesn't carry; reconciled by the cross-provider table, #52).
